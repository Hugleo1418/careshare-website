The idea

People who care, caring for each other.

01 · Tell your story

Share a little about yourself and the loved one you support, without publishing sensitive information.

02 · Find your people

Explore local connections and build trust through conversation and introductions.

03 · Give time

Our proposed hours system recognises the time members give to one another.

04 · Get time back

In the future, earned hours could help you arrange support when you need a break.
